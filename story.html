Our Story

One family, two generations, over three decades of jewellery.

Our Family Legacy

Built on trust,
carried forward.

In 1994, Jay Naidoo opened the doors of Style Jewellers with a promise built on craftsmanship, trust and a passion for fine jewellery. What started as a jewellery store grew into a family legacy — pieces that carry meaning, celebrate milestones and become part of a family's story.

In 2018 the next chapter began when Jay's son, Rev Naidoo, joined the business — fresh ideas and modern creativity, alongside the same dedication to quality. Two generations working side by side, serving customers who now return with their own children.

Today, Style Jewellers is Jay & Co Jewellers — one family, two generations, over three decades of jewellery. Continuing the legacy, one piece at a time.
