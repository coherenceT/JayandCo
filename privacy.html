Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Who we are

Jay & Co Jewellers (“we”, “us”) is a family jewellery workshop and showroom at Office Nr 16B, Smokey Mountain N4 Business Park, Emalahleni. This policy explains what we do with personal information you share with us through this website, our showroom, or by correspondence.

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry or subscribe to our journal, we collect your name, email address and the message you write. We collect no payment details through this website, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, to fulfil a service you requested, and — if you opted in — to send the occasional collection letter. We never sell, rent or trade your information.

How long we keep it

Enquiry correspondence is retained for up to 24 months so we can follow up on ongoing work. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe, which you can do from the footer of any email we send.

Sharing & security

Information is shared only with the service providers needed to operate our inbox and mailing list, each bound by their own data-processing terms. This site is served over HTTPS with a strict Content Security Policy.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the data we hold about you, ask for corrections, or ask us to delete it. Write to info@stylejewellers.co.za and we will respond within 30 days.
